## Learned

- Inherited confirmation did not fire on the summary: the rule needs every input `derived_from`,
  and the layout and histology files are raw inputs the upstream projects also bound (identical
  hashes). Backlog L5 widened: raw-but-identical and `role: reference` inputs should not defeat it.
- `param.agent_proposed` fires on any departure from the pipeline default, not from the tissue
  recommendation; with per-tissue recommendations proposed on every project that is two holds per
  project. Batch review covers confirm holds only. Backlog L6.
- The numeric-claims gate tokenises the digits of `24 h` and `48-2-Liver`; the prompt must say so
  (method `PLAN.md` 1.10). The gate did exactly what it is for: a replicate that quoted things it
  had not cited was refused, and the trace keeps the attempt.
- A split step's output is not a deliverable, so a QC re-run resplits (one to six minutes per
  region). The owner asked why; method `PLAN.md` 3.3 proposes delivering the punch bundles.
- Sibling columns and clipped bands changed the rationales as intended: reviewers cited
  `sibling_concerning_beyond` on the spleen and lung pairs and named the clipped liver band instead
  of explaining a negative edge.
