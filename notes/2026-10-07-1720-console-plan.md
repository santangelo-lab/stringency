## Learned

- From the liver trace: holds were open 21 min 52 s of a 58 min run (confirm 6:49, parameters
  2:11, 1:20 and 0:56, the batch consensus 6:06, the item hold 4:30); compute 14 min; the two
  dispatches about 18 min. The person's attention, not the machine, bounds a single organ; four
  organs make it arrive in bursts.
- The rationale behind `rationale_ref` on the liver's parameter holds is a paragraph the person
  never saw on the page (L8 matters more than its "small" label).
- `PRAGMA data_version` is the cheap, trigger-free way for a reader to notice a trace changed.
- Positional `/p/<i>/` routes cannot survive live discovery; `project_id` is already in
  `stringency.yml`.
- Modules declare `resources: {cpus, memory, timeout}` (module contract), so the executor can claim
  a slot without a new field.
- `/lab/projects/Lyons_CLP` has 26 projects on the board; a fleet view must fold delivered ones.
