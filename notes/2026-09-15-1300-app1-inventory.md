## Done

Platform and format. Xenium, eight region bundles under `/lab/raw/2026-08_xenium_lyons-clp/`
(one per slide and organ), each with `cells.parquet`, `transcripts.parquet`, boundaries,
`cell_feature_matrix.h5`, morphology images, vendor `analysis/`, `metrics_summary.csv`,
`experiment.xenium`, `gene_panel.json`. Panel `mAtlas_v1` with the custom add-on `mMulti_100g`,
5,175 targets in `gene_panel.json`. `experiment.xenium` records `analysis_sw_version
xenium-4.0.2.2`, pixel size 0.2125; the plan's "XOA 6.1" should be checked against this and
corrected if it was the instrument version. Raw counts are the vendor matrix; segmentation is
final for liver and spleen and will be redone with Proseg for lung and gut (decision 2).

Processing already done. Vendor onboard analysis only. Nothing is an input beyond the bundles.

Experimental structure. Six wild-type animals, all CLP, no sham. Time points 6, 24, 48 h, two
animals each. Four organs per animal. Gut, spleen, and lung have two 2 mm punches per animal (a
and b, different sites); liver has one. Two TMAs at 1 mm pitch, one animal per row, seven columns.
TMA 1 = slide 0076581 (animals 6-1, 6-2, 24-1); TMA 2 = slide 0076570 (24-2, 48-1, 48-2), from
`metrics_summary.csv` `cassette_name`. Each organ region on a slide therefore holds six punches
(three for liver): 42 punches in all, transcribed in `/lab/projects/Lyons_CLP/tma_layout.csv`.
Slide is partially confounded with time point: 6 h only on TMA 1, 48 h only on TMA 2, 24 h on
both. Vendor region metrics: gut 285k to 310k cells, lung 209k to 214k, spleen 667k to 748k,
liver 85k on TMA 1 but 26k on TMA 2 with a third of the region area, so one or more of the 24-2,
48-1, 48-2 liver punches may be damaged or missing; histology QC will say. No obs columns exist
beyond the vendor `cells.parquet` schema; the punch, animal, time point, and slide columns come
from the layout table after the split.

The biological replicate. The animal, n = 2 per time point, with punches a and b nested within
animal (owner, 2026-09-15). The owner's intent: see what can be detected at this power, judge
whether more animals are needed, then explore punch-level analysis as hypothesis generation, said
so in the methods. The QC project declares no contrasts; downstream projects declare them.

Positive control. Not applicable to the QC pipeline; the annotation module later needs one.

Existing code. `ROSC_MTA2` on BMESEQ, surveyed in `spec/plans/app1-spatial-qc-plan.md` section 2;
the splitter is `jrose835/Xen_TMA_pipeline`; resegmentation is the ROSC Nextflow Proseg run.

Environments. ROSC conda locks under `setup/envs/`; no spatial image yet. `/data/lab/env` on
PROTSEQ holds `cellpose-opt` and `cellpose3-opt` with lock files and `refgenomes`; the shared
image directory `/data/lab/env/images/` does not exist yet.

Storage. Raw on PROTSEQ, 115 GiB, checksummed (`PROVENANCE.txt`). Project directory
`/lab/projects/Lyons_CLP/` holds the two documents, `README.md`, and `tma_layout.csv`.
