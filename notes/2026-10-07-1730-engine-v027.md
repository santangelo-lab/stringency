## Learned

- `next_step` is called by the reading verb `next`, so the skip is decided there but written only by
  `run` (DECISIONS); the plan note had the transition in `next_step`.
- A fork's inherited step has no action in the child run; anything that reads an inherited step's
  parameters must follow `inherited_from` (`when.admitted_params`).
- Engine-run steps get their job on stdin and leave no `job.json`; to rerun a module by hand,
  rebuild the job from the run's `artifacts` rows.
